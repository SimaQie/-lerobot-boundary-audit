## Review findings addressed in 0.4.1

1. **Reader row labels could be silently truncated.** Both drivers converted
   `.item()` to `int` before comparison. A returned row label `0.9` could match
   expected `0`. Labels must now be Python integers after tensor scalar extraction;
   floats (including `0.0`), booleans and nonfinite values are rejected explicitly.
2. **A duplicated wrong neighbour could hide a definite mismatch.** If samples
   `n-1` and `n+1` are identical but differ from `n`, matching both excludes the
   requested frame. This now produces `wrong_frame`. If `n` is among the exact
   matches, the result remains ambiguous. These are ordinal-reference conclusions,
   not original sensor identities.
3. **Flattening/reshaping could conceal incorrect action axes.** Both drivers now
   check shape as well as values. Expected shape comes from the synthetic oracle
   or stored action rows, with a window dimension only in window modes. This does
   not certify that the stored shape agrees with `info.features.action.shape` or
   that a particular training policy will accept it.
4. **A status string alone did not expose missing query evidence.** Ordinary
   probes now independently reconstruct requested `(row, camera, offset)`
   multiplicities, including padding, repeated random reads and persistent epochs.
   Missing/extra slots or an empty PASS cannot certify a completed probe.

New regression controls cover fractional labels, equal flattened actions with
different axes, repeated wrong neighbours, missing/duplicated evidence, ambiguity
preservation, random/persistent multiplicity and isolated driver imports.

## Improvement: time strata and explicit observability

`probe-loader --sampling stratified` allocates the same row budget across time
strata **within the selected episodes**, using a fixed seed before decoding any
image. It keeps the existing evenly spaced episode selection. It does not sample
every episode, adapt to easier images, retry until PASS, or replace boundary
checks. The default `mixed` sampler preserves the earlier boundary-oriented plan.

Reports now show dataset/selected rows, unique query/window rows, sampled episode
IDs, requested/observed slots and missing/extra evidence. Per-camera counts separate
exact/heuristic matches and ambiguity reasons. Repeated padding/repeated reads
remain individual slots; these counts are not whole-dataset frame coverage.

See [same-budget mixed/stratified results](results/v041/README.md). Both samplers
must be reported together: a more favourable sample cannot erase the earlier
uncertainty or establish a lower defect rate. Small, fixed-seed observations do
not establish statistical superiority.

## Remaining scope

- CPU/Windows real-data checks and CPU/Linux CI do not establish GPU correctness.
- The ordinary reference is PyAV/FFmpeg-based, shares RGB conversion with PyAV,
  and has no recording-time marker. Near-match RGB thresholds remain heuristic.
- Frame/array/copy budgets do not cap total process RAM, total hashing I/O or the
  storage preflight's scan cost on a very large dataset.
- Broader format/schema readiness belongs in complementary validators. Policy
  training gains, physical synchronization and semantic action timing require
  separate experiments and appropriate ground truth.
- No unmodified upstream dataset defect is asserted by this review. The concrete
  defects fixed above were in this project's validators.
