Limits

Fixture markers are synthetic ground truth; real RGB is compared with an independent sequential PyAV PTS/duration reference and a same-environment CPU TorchCodec reference. CUDA output observed in this environment is TV-range RGB, so raw and limited-to-full metrics are reported separately. Performance is a 32-frame single-video decoder microbenchmark, not a full tool or RGB-equivalent throughput claim. This is decode-path evidence, not physical synchronization, training uplift, or a stock LeRobot GPU-reader claim.
