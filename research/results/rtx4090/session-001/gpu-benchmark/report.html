Limits

Fixture markers are synthetic ground truth; real RGB is compared with an independent sequential PyAV reference and a same-environment CPU TorchCodec reference. CUDA output observed in this environment is TV-range RGB, so raw and limited-to-full metrics are reported separately. This is decode-path evidence, not physical synchronization, training uplift, or a stock LeRobot GPU-reader claim.
